# Titan Recovery Tracker

A lightweight recovery tracking resource designed for people using cold exposure, cold plunge routines, and wellness recovery practices consistently.

---

## Features

- Session tracking
- Temperature logging
- Recovery notes
- Consistency monitoring
- Wellness routine organization

---

## Why Tracking Matters

Many people underestimate how important consistency is when building a long-term recovery routine. Tracking sessions helps identify patterns related to energy, recovery, sleep quality, and adaptation over time.

---

## Suggested Data To Track

| Metric | Example |
|---|---|
| Water temperature | 39°F |
| Session duration | 4 minutes |
| Time of day | Morning |
| Recovery notes | Improved post-workout recovery |

---

## Long-Term Consistency

Cold exposure routines often become more effective when sessions are tracked consistently over weeks and months rather than judged after only a few sessions.
